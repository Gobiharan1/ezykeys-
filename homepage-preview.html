How it works

Same-Week Rule
Offer applies only when multiple lessons are scheduled and completed within the same calendar week.
Discount Application
You will pay the standard rate for your first lesson, and the total accumulated weekly discount will be automatically applied to your second (or third) lesson.

We value our instructors’ time. Any cancellations or reschedules with less than 24 hours’ notice will immediately void the weekly discount for that week.
