Learner Driver Lessons

Service 01

Learner Driver Lessons

Whether you've never touched the wheel or have a handful of supervised hours, our learner lessons are designed to meet you exactly where you are. We start with the basics — vehicle controls, observation, road positioning — and progressively build your skills and confidence over time.

Every lesson is structured and purposeful. You'll never just "drive around" — each session has clear objectives and you'll leave knowing exactly what you improved and what to focus on next.

✓
Vehicle controls and basic handling
✓
Road rules and VicRoads requirements
✓
Logbook hours signed each lesson
✓
Progressive lesson structure
✓
Honest post-lesson feedback
Best for: Complete beginners, learners at any stage, young drivers getting started.

Drive Test Preparation

Service 02

Drive Test Preparation

The VicRoads drive test has specific requirements that catch many learners off guard. Our test preparation lessons focus precisely on what the assessor is looking for — smooth observation, correct positioning, safe decision-making, and all the technical elements that earn you a pass.

We drive on the actual VicRoads test routes in your area, and we run mock tests so you know exactly what to expect. There's no better way to walk into test day with real confidence.

✓
Practice on actual VicRoads test routes
✓
Full mock drive test with feedback
✓
Criteria-by-criteria coaching
✓
Common mistakes and how to avoid them
✓
Pre-test warm-up session available
Best for: Learners approaching 120 hours, people who've previously failed, those wanting confidence before test day.

Parking & Manoeuvres

Service 03

Parking & Manoeuvres

Reverse parking, 3-point turns, parallel parking, angle parking — these are the skills that make many learners sweat, but they're absolutely achievable with the right coaching. We break each manoeuvre into clear, repeatable steps that quickly become second nature.

We can run dedicated sessions focused entirely on manoeuvres if that's what you need, or work them into your regular lessons as your skills develop.

✓
Reverse parallel parking
✓
3-point turns (K turns)
✓
Angle parking and bay parking
✓
Uphill and downhill parking
✓
Step-by-step instruction for each skill
Best for: Learners who struggle with parking, anyone wanting to target specific technical weaknesses.

Defensive Driving

Service 04

Defensive Driving Skills

Passing your licence test is just the beginning. Defensive driving teaches you to anticipate hazards, manage risk, and make smart decisions in real-world conditions — the skills that actually keep you safe over a lifetime of driving.

We cover hazard perception, following distances, speed management, merging safely, and how to handle unexpected situations. These aren't just test skills — they're life skills.

✓
Hazard perception and scanning techniques
✓
Safe following distances and space management
✓
Merging, overtaking, and freeway driving
✓
Managing distractions and fatigue
✓
Wet weather and low-visibility driving
Best for: Any learner wanting real-world skills, parents wanting extra safety confidence for their child.

Night Driving

Service 05

Night Driving Lessons

Victoria's learner driver requirements include 10 hours of night driving in your logbook — and for good reason. Driving after dark requires different skills: reading reduced visibility, using headlights correctly, managing fatigue, and adjusting to changed road conditions.

Our dedicated night driving sessions cover all of this in a calm, guided environment. We make sure you're comfortable and competent before signing off your hours.

✓
Counts toward your 10 required night hours
✓
Headlight use and visibility management
✓
Reading the road in low-light conditions
✓
Managing fatigue and alertness
✓
Evening and flexible time slots available
Best for: Learners needing to complete night logbook hours, anyone wanting confidence driving after dark.

Overseas Licence Conversion

Service 06

Overseas Licence Conversion

If you've moved to Victoria with a foreign licence, navigating the local road rules, roundabouts, and VicRoads assessment process can feel overwhelming. We specialise in helping overseas drivers adapt quickly and pass their Victorian licence test with confidence.

We tailor lessons to your specific background — whether you've driven for years overseas or are relatively new to driving. The pace is set by you, and we work around your schedule.

✓
Victorian road rules explained clearly
✓
Left-hand traffic adjustment if needed
✓
VicRoads assessment preparation
✓
Flexible pacing for experienced drivers
✓
Test booking guidance included
Best for: New arrivals to Victoria, international students, expats with foreign driving experience.
