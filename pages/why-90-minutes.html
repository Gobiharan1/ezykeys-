How It Works

How Your 90 Minutes Actually Break Down

Every minute is deliberate. Here's what a typical 90-minute lesson looks like from start to finish.

0–10
Phase 1 · Settle In

Warm-Up & Pre-Drive Chat

We debrief the last lesson, set today's goals, and ease you into the car. No pressure — you're in the zone before you even touch the wheel.

10–75
Phase 2 · Active Driving

Skills Practice & Real Roads

65 uninterrupted minutes of guided driving — enough time to tackle 4–6 skills, make mistakes, get corrected, and actually lock in the improvement. This is where real learning happens.

75–90
Phase 3 · Lock It In

Debrief & Progress Review

We wrap up with honest, actionable feedback — what you nailed, what to work on next, and a clear picture of where you are on the path to your licence.

The Case for 90 Minutes

8 Reasons It Makes a Difference

These aren't opinions — they're patterns we've seen across hundreds of students over 15 years of teaching.

01
⏱️

More real driving time

Setup and debrief take roughly the same time regardless of lesson length. With 90 minutes, you get 65+ min of actual driving — not 50.

02
🚀

Faster skill progress

Muscle memory forms through repetition. 90 minutes gives you enough passes at each skill for it to start feeling natural — not just familiar.

03
💪

Confidence builds faster

Anxious learners need time to relax before they can truly absorb anything. A longer session means they actually settle in and drive freely.

04
🔄

Master the tricky stuff

Parallel parking, roundabouts, merging — you can't just run these once and call it done. 90 minutes lets you run them until they click.

05
🎯

Get test-ready sooner

Longer sessions simulate the sustained focus required on test day. You build the mental stamina for the drive test — not just individual skills.

06
💰

Better value per session

More driving time, more feedback, stronger improvement per dollar. Two 45-minute lessons cost more — and teach less — than one 90-minute session.

07
🗓️

Easier to schedule

One weekly 90-minute lesson is far easier to fit into a busy life than two or three shorter ones. Less logistics, more consistency.

08
🧠

Fewer total lessons needed

Students who train in 90-minute sessions consistently reach test-readiness faster than those in shorter sessions. Less overall spend, quicker outcome.

Is It Right For You?

Who Benefits Most from 90 Minutes

Honestly — almost everyone. But here's a breakdown.

✅

90 Minutes is ideal if you…

✓
Are a complete beginner needing time to settle
✓
Feel nervous or anxious behind the wheel
✓
Want to build real confidence, not just tick boxes
✓
Have specific skills to master (parking, roundabouts)
✓
Are preparing for your drive test and want to simulate real conditions
✓
Have a busy schedule and want maximum progress per session
🤔

You might consider 60 minutes if…

!
You only need a quick refresher before a specific situation
!
You're an experienced driver just needing a local road orientation
!
You have a physical limitation that makes longer sessions tiring
!
You're on a very tight budget and one skill is all you need to address

Not sure? Give us a call and we'll honestly recommend what's best for your situation — no upsell, just straight advice.
